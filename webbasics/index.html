welcome to my page.

Hello! My name is Amaya Williams, and this page is all about me. I was loath to make another page dedicated to my resume, so I decided to make one about my hobbies and interests instead.

I was born and raised in Harlem and currently still live here with my cat, Ollie. I attended Northeastern University and graduated in 2022 with a degree in Journalism. After three years in the industry, I decided to return to school for a Master’s in Library and Information Science.

Though writing about myself is consistently pretty difficult, I’ve taken pride in discovering who I am and what makes me tick. I’ll take any personality test offered online (I’m INTJ, type AB, and a Slytherin), I love to collect things (seashells, Playbills, and ticket stubs), and I humor New Age practices (I am an Aquarius and pulled the ‘Death’ card for 2026). Above all else, I spend a lot of time organizing, which is most likely why I’m currently pursuing a degree in organizing and classifying. I make lists about all sorts of things and firmly believe things will disappear if I don’t write them down.

To that point, this website is evidence I existed this year.
