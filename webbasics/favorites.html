one painting


Pictured left is "At the Circus Fernando: Medrano with a Piglet" painted by Henri de Toulouse-Lautrec somewhere between 1884 and 1894.

I discovered this painting after visiting the Art Institute of Chicago the year before last. The museum had a collection of circus-related paintings, in which de Toulouse-Lautrec was heavily featured (among others such as Joan Miró and Pierre-Auguste Renoir). The collection triggered my interest in this specific subject area of clown- and carnival-related paintings and ephemera. Life is a circus after all!

Generally speaking, my favorite art movements are American Genre and German Expressionism; therefore, the references I have on non-area-specific 19th-century circus art is very random––even to me.
